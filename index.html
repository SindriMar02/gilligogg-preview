01 / WHERE THE NAME COMES FROM

Kjarval shouted it
coming down the hill

He used it whenever something delighted him, and when people asked what it meant he told them it was pure Icelandic. He had made it up. It stood for everything good, and hello, and hurrah, and bravo, all at once.

„Gilligogg, gilligogg!“

Jóhannes S. Kjarval MORGUNBLAÐIÐ · 8 SEPT 1965

The bar opened on Austurstræti in June 2024, took the painter's word for a name, and hung the walls with four large oil paintings by Þrándur Þórarinsson. Reykjavík as it looked early last century, crowded with faces from the city's cultural life, then and now.
